How we work

A principal, not a broker

NMLE Properties LLC is a limited liability company authorised to transact business in the State of Florida under document number M20000005028, with registration details on public record with the Florida Division of Corporations.

We buy and sell our own land, which means we are not real estate brokers and charge no commission to anyone. It also means our interests and a seller's are not aligned — get your own attorney and your own valuation. Everything we publish about a parcel comes from public records and is a starting point, not a warranty: no representation is made that any parcel can be built on, connected to utilities or permitted for a septic system. Survey it, examine title, and ask the county yourself.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Why is this parcel so cheap?

Usually one of five reasons: no legal access, wetland, a flood designation, zoning that permits little, or utilities a long way off. We research all five and tell you which applies — that is the whole point of asking.

Can I build a house on it?

We will not promise that, ever. Buildability depends on zoning, access, septic percolation, wetland extent and the permitting authority's decision. Ask the county before you buy, not after.

Are you real estate agents?

No. We buy and sell our own land as principal, represent nobody and charge no commission. If you are selling to us, use your own attorney — our interests are not yours.

Do I need a survey?

Yes. Boundaries, encroachments and access are routinely different from what a parcel map suggests, and a survey is cheap against the cost of being wrong.

What about the taxes owing?

On parcels we sell, taxes are current at closing. If you are selling us land with back taxes, say so — it is common and it is not a dealbreaker.

Do you offer land as an investment?

No. We sell parcels, not investments, and nothing here is an offer of any security, fund or land banking scheme.

Got a parcel number?

Send it and we will tell you what the public records show. No commission either way.
