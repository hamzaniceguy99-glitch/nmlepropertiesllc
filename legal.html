The company buys and sells land for its own account as principal. It is not a licensed real estate broker or sales associate, does not market or sell land belonging to others, does not represent any buyer or seller and charges no commission. Information provided about a parcel, including zoning, permitted use, access, easements, utility availability, flood zone designation, wetland extent, soil or percolation suitability and buildability, is compiled from public records and third-party sources, is provided for general information, and is not warranted as accurate or complete. Every buyer is strongly encouraged to conduct independent due diligence, including a survey, title examination, environmental and wetland assessment and enquiry to the county and the permitting authority, and to instruct their own attorney before purchase.
