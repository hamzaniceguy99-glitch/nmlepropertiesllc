Program 02 · The reasons it is cheap

Research & disclosure

Five things decide whether a parcel is worth anything: legal access, zoning and permitted use, utility availability, flood designation and wetland extent. We research all five from public records and share what we find — including the findings that reduce what a parcel is worth.

Program 03 · Verify everything yourself

Selling parcels

We tell buyers what we found and then tell them to check it themselves — a survey, a title examination, and a call to the county and the permitting authority. Our research is a starting point compiled from public records, not a warranty, and anyone selling land who implies otherwise should concern you.

Program 04 · Taxes and mowing

Holding & maintenance

Vacant land accumulates problems quietly: unpaid taxes heading toward a tax deed sale, overgrowth triggering a code violation, and illegal dumping that becomes the owner's removal bill. Parcels we hold are kept current, which is also why they are cleaner than most land that comes to market.
